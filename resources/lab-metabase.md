<h1>Lab Metabase</h1>

<p class="page-intro">
Internal lab resources, including patient databases, biobank records, grants, and shared documents, are available only to members of the lab through the BCM intranet.
</p>

- **GHI_Dashboard**  
  Available only on the BCM intranet.  
  This dashboard integrates a software ecosystem providing centralized access to the patient database, sequencing data, lab ordering system, data backup, and other internal tools.

- **WES/WGS GUI**  
  Available only on the BCM intranet.

- **WES/WGS CRAM/CRAI server for local IGV**  
  Available only on the BCM intranet.

- **Lab Sample Metabase**  
  Please access through the Dashboard
